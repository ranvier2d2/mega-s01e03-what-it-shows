Fixing the parser closes only the first ticket. The other two keep coming in after the fix.

The three bugs

parser: the shared parser reads day-first dates month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. Any date with a day of twelve or less comes out with day and month swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. Reports from the app, from email and from the booking-v2 form land here.

hint: booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to type month first. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way. The input is already wrong, so a fixed parser still flips it. Only reports through the booking-v3 form, from the tenants that run it.

shift: dates move by one day. A swap moves a date by months. These reports say “Tuesday 12 May” became “Monday 11 May”, “every booking in June is one day off”, and “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” That is a time zone conversion, not the parser. The desk had already read them as a one-day move (desk-notes.json); now they count as their own cause.

How they were counted

  • The export lists some reports twice. One id, one report.
  • The cause comes from the report’s tenant, its booking form and its channel (causes.ts), not from the intake tag. Intake guessed from the reporter’s browser; the column “tagged as” shows how often it guessed the other bug.
  • shift comes first: a report the desk read as a one-day move is shift whatever its tenant.

Lessons

  • Matching on subject line (decision 0001) merged three bugs into one ticket. Read the bodies before you merge.
  • “Known” is a claim about one cause. Check it against the table before you close the queue.
